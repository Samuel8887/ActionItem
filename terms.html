Using Together

Together is a shared action item tracker for your group. Sign in with your own email address and only confirm work you have completed.

Every person listed in the group must complete their part before an action item moves to Completed. Undoing your own completion reopens the action item. Group rosters are fixed in this version.

The operator manages the deployment and database. For support, contact LJGV@novonordisk.com.
