Data information

Together stores account email addresses, group rosters, action item titles and descriptions, due dates, and individual completion timestamps in the Supabase project configured by this website’s operator.

Signed-in group members can see their own group’s roster and action items. Authentication is provided by Supabase. A sign-in session may be stored in your browser to keep you signed in. Passwords are handled by the authentication provider, not stored in the action item tables.

The site loads the Supabase and Microsoft Teams client libraries from external CDNs. Teams is a container for this app; this version does not read your Teams channel membership or use Microsoft single sign-on.

For questions about access, account deletion, retention, or removal of stored data, contact LJGV@novonordisk.com.
